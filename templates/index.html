Watch the Magic

The Art of Close-Up

Close-up magic is the most intimate branch of the conjuring arts. There is no stage, no distance, no curtain to hide behind. The magician stands among the audience — cards, coins, and borrowed objects passing through hands that are always visible. It demands a different kind of performance than stage illusions: presence, timing, and genuine connection with every single person watching.

Built for Your Event

What We Do

Close-Up Magic That Moves with Your Guests

Josiah specializes in close-up and parlor magic — the kind that happens at the table, in the middle of the cocktail hour, or right in the palm of your guest's hand. Unlike stage shows that require a seated audience and a fixed schedule, Josiah roams and mingles, bringing the magic directly to people in small, intimate groups wherever they happen to be standing.

This format is uniquely effective at events where you want to energize the room, break the ice, and give guests something to talk about long after the night is over. Corporate events, weddings, private parties, galas, product launches, and intimate dinner parties — Josiah has performed them all and knows exactly how to read each environment.

Each booking includes a pre-event consultation to understand the occasion, the audience, and any specific moments Josiah can enhance. The result is a performance that feels completely native to your event — not a generic add-on, but the highlight of the evening.
