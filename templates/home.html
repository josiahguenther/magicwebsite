About

A Refined Goofball

Josiah does the impossible. Sort of.

At only 15 years old, Josiah is already a talented performer, known for his confident performance, clean sleight of hand, and quirky humor. He is a member of the exclusive Junior Society of the Academy of the Magical Arts (better known as the legendary Magic Castle) where he continues to hone his skills under the mentorship of some of the greatest magicians of our era. In addition to being a talented magician, Josiah is also an award-winning public speaker, who placed 3rd in original oratory at speech and debate nationals, among many other awards. That means that his talkification is very gooderfied. He combines his skill for speaking well with his magical skills to make truly memorable performances. Whatever services you may require, Josiah is ready to entertain, captivate, and inspire his next audience and ultimately bring a bit more wonder to this wonderless world.
