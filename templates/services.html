Shows and Performances for Your Event

Josiah is a magical performer whose talents will benefit a wide variety of events. Whether it be corporate events, weddings, private parties, galas, product launches, or intimate dinner parties, Josiah is ready to entertain and amaze you and your guests. He offers multiple kinds of performances (detailed below) depending on your event.

🎩

Full Show

A full magic show that can be customized in length and scope to fit the needs of your event. Whether it’s a group of 20 or a crowd of 500, Josiah will be sure to amaze and astound with a full polished performance, featuring sleight of hand, comedy, and probably snacks.

💼

Walkaround Magic

Josiah will interact directly with the guests at your event, performing magic right under their noses, at tables or in small groups. Unlike stage shows that require a seated audience and a fixed schedule, Josiah roams and mingles, bringing the magic directly to people in small, intimate groups wherever they happen to be standing.

✨

Customized to Your Event

Whatever your event is, adding Josiah’s magic is sure to make it better. If your needs don't fit into these categories, we’d be happy to talk and try to accommodate them.
