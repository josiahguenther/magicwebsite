Shows and Performances for Your Event

Josiah is a magical performer whose talents will benefit a wide variety of events. Whether it be corporate events, weddings, private parties, galas, product launches, or intimate dinner parties, Josiah is ready to entertain and amaze you and your guests. He offers multiple kinds of performances (detailed below) depending on your event.

🎩

Full Show

A full magic show that can be customized in length and scope to fit the needs of your event. Whether it’s a group of 20 or a crowd of 500, Josiah will be sure to amaze and astound with a full polished performance, featuring sleight of hand, comedy, and probably snacks.

💼

Walkaround Magic

Josiah will interact directly with the guests at your event, performing magic right under their noses, at tables or in small groups. Unlike stage shows that require a seated audience and a fixed schedule, Josiah roams and mingles, bringing the magic directly to people in small, intimate groups wherever they happen to be standing.

✨

Customized to Your Event

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? Here are answers to some of the things I'm asked most often. If you don't see your question here, feel free to contact me.

What types of events do you perform at?

I perform at a wide variety of events, including corporate gatherings, private parties, churches, schools, banquets, fundraisers, weddings, and community events. Whether you're entertaining a small group or a large audience, I can help create an unforgettable experience.

What kind of magic do you perform?

My performances combine astonishing sleight of hand, interactive audience participation, and clean comedy. I specialize in close-up magic performed right in your guests' hands, as well as engaging stage performances for larger audiences.

How long are your shows?

My full shows are about twenty minutes on average. However, I always have to cut a lot of material from those shows, so my performances can easily be customized to fit your schedule.

Do you travel?

I travel up to an hour drive from my home in La Mirada, California, for no extra cost and can often accommodate events farther away as well. If you're unsure whether I serve your area, just ask.

Is your show appropriate for children?

Absolutely. Every performance is family-friendly and I also have a special show created specifically with an audience of children in mind.

How far in advance should I book?

Popular dates—especially weekends and holidays—can fill quickly. I recommend booking as early as possible, though you're always welcome to inquire about last-minute availability.

How much does a magic show cost?

Pricing depends on the type of performance, audience size, location, and duration. Contact me with your event details, and I'll be happy to provide a personalized quote.

What do you need at the venue?

Most performances require very little setup. For larger stage shows, I'll discuss any sound, lighting, or space requirements with you beforehand so everything runs smoothly.

How do I book a performance?

Simply visit the Contact page, send me a message with your event details, and I'll get back to you as soon as possible to discuss availability and the best entertainment option for your event.

Does Pineapple Belong on Pizza?

Okay, that's not really a frequently asked question, but yes, it most certainly does. It's tasty and nutritionally beneficial.
