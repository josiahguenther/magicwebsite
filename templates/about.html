About Josiah

A Refined Goofball

In a world of social media, smartphones, and AI, it’s easy to feel as if you have all the answers. So maybe it’s not more answers that you need, but rather a few more questions. A few more moments that make you stop and say “How?” A little more wonder. And that’s where Josiah comes in.

At only 15 years old, Josiah is already an outstanding magician whose talents far exceed his age. His interest in magic began at age 13, when a friend showed him a simple card trick. Magic quickly turned from a casual hobby into a passion. Now, three years, and countless youtube tutorials, books, and performances later, Josiah continues to dedicate most of his time, effort, and money to improving at the art of magic.

When he was just 14 years old, he became a member of the exclusive Junior Society of the Academy of the Magical Arts (better known as the legendary Magic Castle) where he continues to hone his skills under the mentorship of some of the greatest magicians of our era.

In addition to being a talented magician, Josiah is also an award winning public speaker, who placed 3rd in original oratory at speech and debate nationals, among many other awards. That means that his talkification is very gooderfied. He combines his skill for speaking well with his magical skills to make truly memorable performances.

Whether He's performing for a corporate banquet, church, school assembly, wedding reception, or private celebration, the goal is always the same: to create genuine moments of wonder that bring people together.

As G.K. Chesterton once put it, "The world will never starve for want of wonders, but only for want of wonder." Josiah will show you the wonders. It's up to you to bring the wonder.
