About Josiah

A Refined Goofball

I do the impossible. Sort of.

At only 15 years old, I have already had the privilege of entertaining audiences throughout Southern California with both close-up and stage magic. My performances combine elegant sleight of hand, audience interaction, comedy, and storytelling to create moments that people remember long after the show is over.

I am a proud member of the Junior Society of the Academy of Magical Arts—the legendary Magic Castle in Hollywood—where I continue to study under some of the finest magicians in the world. Performing at the Castle has challenged me to pursue excellence in every aspect of my craft.

Outside of magic, I am also an award-winning public speaker, placing 3rd in Original Oratory at the National Invitational Tournament of Champions. Those speaking skills allow me to connect naturally with audiences, keeping every performance engaging, polished, and full of personality.

Whether I'm performing for a corporate banquet, church, school assembly, wedding reception, or private celebration, my goal is always the same: to create genuine moments of wonder that bring people together.

Magic has an incredible ability to make us stop, laugh, question what we thought was possible, and share an unforgettable experience with the people around us. That's why I love performing—and why I would be honored to bring that experience to your event.
