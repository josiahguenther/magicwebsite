For the text on the website:
Go to the templates folder and in each of the html files you can scroll down and replace the white text in the document.
The three html files of interest are the home.html, services.html, and contact.html

For the images:
The home and services page both have images. Just put the files jpg image files in the images folder in the static
folder
The top of the home page is: hero-home.jpg
The pair of photos in the middle of the page are: home-photo-1.jpg and home-photo-2.jpg
The large image at the top of the services page is: hero-services.jpg
The pair of photos at the bottom of the services page are: services-photo-1.jpg and services-photo-2.jpg

For the social media links:
Go to the base.html file in the templates folder and scroll down to the footer area and change the links to your
channels

To make the contact form work:
The information about your email and the necessary password is stored in the .env file. This is because this file is
secure and so the information stays secret.
You need to change the OWNER_EMAIL, SMTP_USER, and SMTP_PASSWORD.
The OWNER_EMAIL and SMTP_USER are the same. This is just the email you want the message from the contact form to go to.
The SMTP_PASSWORD is a special password generated with your good account. To get it follow these steps:
    On your profile click manage my Google account
    Click on "Security & sign-in"
    Click 2-step verification and enable it
    Scroll down to where it says "App Passwords"
    Create a name for an app -- it can be whatever you want, it doesn't matter
    Then copy the password that it gives you
    Then put that password in the .env file across from the SMTP_PASSWORD
